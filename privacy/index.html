Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Stub Club (cfbstub.club) is a small hobby site for keeping track of the college football teams, stadiums and bowl games you've seen in person. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it. Short version: we keep only what's needed to run your wall, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so you can sign in. If you use Google sign-in, Google also shares your name and profile picture with us.
  • Your profile: the username and settings you choose, including whether your wall is public.
  • Your collection: the teams, stadiums, bowls and matchups you mark.

We don't collect game dates, your location, payment details or contacts. We don't run ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Sign-in uses a small amount of browser storage to keep you logged in.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and save your wall.
  • To show your wall at your public page, but only if you turn that on in Settings. Walls are private by default.
  • To reply if you email us.

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Services we rely on

These companies process data on our behalf to keep the site running:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Netlify: website hosting.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google."

Deleting your data

Email hello@cfbstub.club from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and collection. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Stub Club's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.

Kids

Stub Club isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
